dhcpd is a DHCP server: it hands out addresses to computers on one of its interfaces, which must have a fixed address. Leases are saved, so computers keep their addresses when the server restarts. Run it in the background with "bg" on an advanced computer, or from startup.

The settings dhcpd.lease (seconds, default 3600), dhcpd.dns (DNS servers, separated by commas), dhcpd.domain and dhcpd.gateway are passed on to clients.

ex:
"dhcpd top" hands out .100 to .199 of the network on the interface "top".
"dhcpd top 50 80" hands out .50 to .80.
