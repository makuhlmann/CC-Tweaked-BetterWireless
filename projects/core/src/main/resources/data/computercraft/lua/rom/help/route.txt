route shows the routing table, and sets up routes and routers. Settings are saved, and apply straight away.

ex:
"route" shows every route: where it leads, the router to send to, and its cost.
"route default 10.0.0.1" sends anything for other networks to 10.0.0.1.
"route add 10.5.0.0/16 10.4.0.1" sends anything for 10.5.0.0/16 to 10.4.0.1.
"route delete 10.5.0.0/16" removes that route.
"route forwarding on" makes this computer a router, forwarding packets between its interfaces.
"route rip on" makes this router learn routes from other routers, and tell them its own.
"route rip default" does the same, and tells them this router is the way to everywhere else.
