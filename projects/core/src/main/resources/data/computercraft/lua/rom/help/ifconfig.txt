ifconfig shows this computer's network interfaces, and sets their addresses. Every modem is an interface. Addresses are saved, so they are restored when the computer starts.

ex:
"ifconfig" shows every interface, its address, and how much traffic it has seen.
"ifconfig top 10.0.0.1/24" gives the interface "top" the address 10.0.0.1 on network 10.0.0.0/24.
"ifconfig top none" removes the address of the interface "top".
"ifconfig top band low" makes a wireless modem use the long-range low band after the computer restarts.
